This page describes the current information practices of the Clockles Technology static website.

LAST UPDATED · OCTOBER 2026

The website is designed to provide company and service information without collecting form submissions.

Information sent by email

Links on this website may open your email application to contact Clockles Technology. If you choose to send an email, the information in that message will be received and handled through the email service you use and Clockles’ email provider.

Website hosting

This website is intended to be hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process standard technical information, such as requests and security logs, to operate and protect the service. Refer to GitHub’s current privacy information for details about its hosting services.

Third-party services

The current site does not embed analytics, advertising pixels, external fonts, or a third-party contact form. This notice should be updated if those services are added or if the website begins collecting information directly.
