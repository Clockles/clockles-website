POSITION 01 · FULL-TIME · SENIOR LEVELSenior Embedded Systems Engineer+

Position Overview

Own embedded architecture and implementation for systems that combine firmware, sensors, electronics, and edge intelligence. Lead development from technical requirements through integration, debugging, and verification.

Key Responsibilities

  • Translate product and system requirements into embedded architecture, interfaces, and implementation plans.
  • Develop and review firmware for microcontrollers and embedded platforms, including drivers, device control, communications, and data acquisition.
  • Integrate sensors and electronic subsystems; diagnose system behavior with appropriate lab and software tools.
  • Evaluate edge and embedded AI approaches against memory, latency, power, and reliability constraints.
  • Lead system bring-up, integration, verification, and root-cause analysis across hardware and software.
  • Set code and design review practices, document technical decisions, and mentor engineers.

Preferred Background & Qualifications

Demonstrated senior-level experience delivering embedded or connected systems. Strong C/C++ and Python skills; hands-on work with microcontrollers, embedded Linux or RTOS, device interfaces, and communication protocols. Experience debugging integrated hardware/software systems and communicating technical tradeoffs.

Tools & Technical Areas

C/C++, Python, microcontrollers, RTOS or embedded Linux, sensor interfaces, communication protocols, version control, and electronic test equipment, selected to fit the project.

Tools & Technical Areas

Python, PyTorch or equivalent ML frameworks, NumPy/SciPy, scikit-learn, OpenCV or audio tooling, experiment tracking, and model evaluation methods.

  • Lead electronic architecture and circuit design for sensor, analog, digital, and mixed-signal systems.

Proven ability to guide design decisions across disciplines and take systems from concept to validated prototype.

Tools & Technical Areas

Schematic and PCB design tools, analog and mixed-signal design methods, oscilloscopes, signal generators, analyzers, and documentation/version-control workflows.
